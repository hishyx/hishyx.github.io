FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here are clear, upfront answers to common inquiries.

Do I need to buy domain and hosting separately?

No, I can handle the setup for you. I will recommend reliable, budget-friendly hosting and domain registrars (like Hostinger or Namecheap) and configure the servers on your behalf.

Will my website look good on mobile phones?

Yes. Over 80% of your local customers will visit your website using their smartphones. Every site I develop is fully responsive, looking premium and fast on all device widths.

Can I edit or update the website content later?

Absolutely. Depending on your needs, I can configure a simple admin panel or guide you on how to easily update pricing lists, pictures, and timing details directly.

How long does the development take?

Starter pages are completed in 5–7 days once all logo, business copy, and images are provided. Professional and Premium sites generally take 10–15 days.
